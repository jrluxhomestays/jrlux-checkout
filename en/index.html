A Note From Your Host

Checkout is at 1:00 PM. Before you depart, we would be grateful for your help with a few final touches:

1
Personal BelongingsKindly ensure all personal items are collected and surfaces left tidy.
2
Used DishesPlease place used dishes and utensils in the kitchen sink — no washing needed.
3
RubbishPlease place rubbish in the bins provided.
4
Used TowelsPlease place used towels in the bathroom — no washing needed.
5
RefrigeratorKindly take along or discard any perishable food or drinks.
6
Energy ConservationKindly switch off lights, air-conditioning, fans, and the water heater.
7
Lift Access CardPlease leave it on the tray by the umbrella stand.
8
Parking Access CardIf applicable, please hand it to your host waiting on the left side of the exit gate.

Pay It Forward (Optional)

At JRLUX Homestays, we embrace a Pay It Forward philosophy, where small gestures contribute to a better experience for future guests. If you wish to support our self-service homestay community, you are welcome to voluntarily:

  • Wash any used dishes or kitchen utensils
  • Refresh used towels using the laundry facilities provided
  • Dispose of rubbish at the designated refuse room

These actions are entirely optional and are not required for checkout.

Thank you for your consideration and for being part of the JRLUX community. We wish you a safe journey and look forward to welcoming you again in the future.
